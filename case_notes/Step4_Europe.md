# Step 4 full sweep — Europe

**Scope:** all 29 European panel countries, 1945–2019. Spain, France, and the UK carry the calibration determinations in `Step4_Trevijano_calibration.md`; they are not silently recoded here.
**Status:** substantive case determinations; user review requested; not merged into the panel.
**Non-contamination:** no economic outcome, V-Dem, or Polity evidence was used. CCP is a candidate ledger, supplemented by formal statutes and institutional acts.

## 1. Accepted power-structure episodes

All positive codes denote partial effective change, not democratic completion. For communist takeovers, (c) denotes new party–state fusion and (d) the removal of effective competitive control; for democratic openings the same dimensions reverse. A constitution that consummates an open process does not reset its effective date.

## 2. Negative and one-dimension determinations

- **BEL:** post-war restoration is the 1945 baseline. The 1968–94 reforms principally reallocate authority vertically and create federal institutions; under the calibration rule this is not automatically (b). Party-finance and later reforms do not establish a second dimension in the same window. No power episode accepted.
- **DNK:** the 1953 Constitution abolished the Landsting (a), altered succession, and changed electoral rules, but no independent second effective dimension is established. Succession rules are not relabelled as new control of rulers. No later two-dimension episode.
- **FIN:** the 1999 Constitution (731/1999), effective 1 March 2000, consolidated and reorganized the formal order without a demonstrated two-dimension effective rupture. Party-financing, franchise, EU, and routine parliamentary amendments do not combine automatically. No power episode accepted.
- **ISL:** electoral district, franchise, judicial, EU/EEA, and rights amendments across 1959–2013 do not produce two qualifying dimensions in any three-year window. No power episode accepted.
- **IRL:** EU referendums, voting-age/access changes, the 1998 settlement, and rights amendments do not establish a two-dimension effective change. Devolution/territorial arrangements are not independence of existing Westminster-style national representation. No power episode accepted.
- **LUX:** electoral/franchise, EU, and institutional amendments do not meet the threshold. The 2008–09 curtailment of the Grand Duke's formal assent role did not transform an existing effective popular control mechanism. No power episode accepted.
- **NLD:** post-war restoration is the 1945 baseline. The 1953/1956 electoral reforms and 1983 general revision do not demonstrate two effective dimensions in one window; later EU and routine amendments do not either. No power episode accepted.
- **NOR:** electoral, parliamentary, judicial, and rights amendments are isolated dimension changes or routine operation of existing checks. No two-dimension episode accepted.
- **CYP 1974:** coup, foreign intervention, and territorial division are recorded as severe effective disruption, but the rapid reversal/restoration does not supply a clean new two-dimension national legal order in the Republic's panel record. It remains a flagged narrative case, not an accepted binary event.
- **CZE/SVK 1960:** formal socialist replacement without a separately established effective two-dimension change beyond PS-01. The 1968 federation is vertical reallocation, not automatically (b).
- **DEU 1968:** emergency-powers amendment is (a) only; 1990 unification is territorial succession/continuity of the FRG order; 2006 federalism is vertical (a) only; 2009 fiscal rules are not power transformation.
- **GRC 1986:** strengthened prime-ministerial/parliamentary arrangements are (a) only. GRC 2001/2008/2019 do not establish a second distinct dimension.
- **HUN 1972:** formal reorganization within the party-state; no second effective episode. HUN 2011 electoral redesign is not counted separately as (b) merely because districting/formula changed.
- **ITA 1953:** electoral premium law is (b) only; 1963 and 1991–93 judicial/electoral changes do not meet two dimensions; 2001 devolution is vertical (a) only; 2005 electoral reform is (b) only.
- **MLT 1974:** republican constitutional change replaced the head of state without changing effective party competition/control; 2014–16 party-finance and institutional changes lack a second dimension.
- **POL 1992/1997:** formal constitutional replacement within the already transformed order; 2006 is one-dimension institutional change. PS-POL-04 does not rest on expert backsliding ratings.
- **ROU 1952/1965:** formal consolidation of the existing party-state; 2003 executive/parliamentary changes principally support (a), without a second independently demonstrated dimension.
- **SVN 1997–2016:** electoral, EU, judicial, and fiscal amendments do not combine into two dimensions in a three-year window.
- **SWE 1974–75:** the new Instrument of Government formalized and extended the existing parliamentary order; monarchy changes did not transform effective control. Party financing alone is not (c) without organizational-integration evidence.
- **CHE:** the 1999 Constitution was a formal total revision with effective institutional continuity; franchise changes are not automatically (b), and judicial/federal reforms do not produce two dimensions in one window. No power episode accepted.

### Apparatus negatives and cautions

- **BEL, DNK, FIN, ISL, IRL, LUX, MLT, NLD, NOR, SWE, CHE:** no additional episode is accepted without two distinct, substantively reorganized components in a three-year window. Departmental renaming, isolated central-bank statutes, routine agency creation, and annual fiscal rules do not suffice. Malta's independence reorganization is AR-MLT-01; the central bank's 1968 creation is not allowed to stretch that episode beyond three years.
- **CYP 1974:** coercive breakdown and foreign intervention are context, but a second durable component under a restored national authority is not established.
- **DEU 1955–56:** Bundeswehr creation is an important coercive change but is not paired with a second component in that window; AR-DEU-01 remains the only accepted German episode.
- **HUN 2010–12 and POL 2015–17:** power-structure findings are not automatically apparatus findings. Their appointment and judicial changes do not by themselves establish two distinct apparatus components.
- **ITA 1946–48:** formal republic and Constitution changed the rules of power, but continuity of the permanent administrative, coercive, and judicial machinery prevents an automatic apparatus code.
- **CZE/SVK:** shared events are not duplicated after 1992. The 1993 division is included as the consummation of the post-1989 apparatus interval, not as an unrelated annual event.

## 5. Verification limits

Several early communist-takeover apparatus episodes use named institutional reorganizations corroborated by constitutional and institutional histories rather than scans of every original decree; they carry medium-high rather than high confidence. AR-AUT-01 is the weakest accepted apparatus case because restoration involved reconstituting existing republican machinery rather than creating new organizations. The accepted code rests on the actual transfer from annexed Nazi machinery, not on the formal proclamation alone. All accepted Hungary/Poland backsliding codes rest on identified laws and appointment structures, never on expert indices.
