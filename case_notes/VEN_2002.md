# Case note — Venezuela–US 2002–2017: the gradual severance

**Episode ID:** E016 | **Status:** calibration episode, in panel (VEN); the
interval-coding test case and the design's crux case | **Coder:** assistant +
user, review pending | **Confidence:** medium-high (interval, contested timing)

## The four judgments (CP §6.1)

**1. Membership (R1), pre-2002.** Venezuela inside the US sphere: F2 — oil
trade, US taking ~50% of exports around 2000 (DOTS: 52.1% in 2000); F1 —
military cooperation programs; F3 — Rio Treaty (ATOP 3075), OAS member.

**2. Severance (R2) — interval [2002, 2017].** The process is gradual, so the
interval carries the coding (CP §6 R2):
- **2002** (earliest defensible): April coup attempt and the Dec 2002–Feb 2003
  oil strike — the political rupture with the US.
- 2004–05: military cooperation ended, US military mission expelled (F1).
- 2006–08: US arms embargo (GSDB 802), financial measures (GSDB 827).
- 2010: ambassadors withdrawn (diplomatic downgrade).
- **2015**: US Executive Order 13692, trade+financial sanctions (GSDB 1051);
  ATOP records Venezuela's Rio Treaty exit in 2015.
- **2017** (latest defensible): August 2017 financial sanctions cut Venezuela
  from US capital markets (GSDB 1098) — the enforced economic break; OAS
  withdrawal announced Apr 2017; EU/Canada join sanctions (GSDB 1095/1096).
Families F1, F2, F3 all terminate, but across 15 years — exactly the case the
interval rule exists for. Endpoint robustness: 2002 / 2017 (midpoint ~2010).

**3. Voluntariness (R4).** **Chosen (mixed: enforcement from 2015).** The
realignment was initiated by Chávez; from 2015–17 the patron enforced and
deepened the break by sanctions (R4's second clause applies to the late phase).
Primary coding follows who initiated (Baltics convention), the mix is flagged.

**4. R5 n/a. R6: 0.** (Reorientation context, not coded as a new entry: ALBA
2004, Russian arms from 2005–06, Chinese loans from 2007 — partial, and no new
patron replaces the US; the UN record confirms no convergence to Russia.)

## Validation

- **UN ideal points:** monotone divergence from the US: −0.18 (1995) → −0.53
  (1999) → −0.88 (2005) → −1.31 (2011) → −1.46 (2015) → −1.71 (2019). The
  *country* moves (unlike the patron-driven divergences of Cuba 1989 and
  Vietnam 1989) — consistent with a chosen severance. Passes.
- **DOTS trade delta (first quantified use):** US share of Venezuelan goods
  exports 52.1% (2000) → 45.2% (2002) → 38.0% (2013) → 36.6% (2017) →
  12.0% (2019). The decline is gradual through the interval, then cliff-like
  after enforcement — it visually confirms both the interval and the 2017
  endpoint.

## Outcome check (post-hoc; the honest hard part)

Two disruption episodes touch this severance:
- **D090 (1997–2003, −22.7%):** the contraction phase (2002–03, −10.4% and
  −9.4%, the strike years) overlaps the interval's start; but the episode's
  onset (1997–2001) is the oil-price decline, *pre-severance*. Caveat recorded.
- **D091 (2011–2019, −60.1% + hyperinflation):** onset 2014 is the oil-price
  crash (external shock), *before* the 2015/2017 enforcement; the deepest years
  (2016–19, −17.6%, −15.1%, −17.6%, −25.4%) follow the enforcement. Mixed
  causation — recorded, not smoothed over.

**Crux cross-reference (for step 4):** by Protocol §4, Venezuela's power
structure was transformed by 2009 at the latest (enabling laws 2000/2007/2013,
TSJ packing 2004, PDVSA purge 2002–03, term limits abolished 2009). The 2011–19
collapse therefore sits in Table 1's **bottom row** (realignment *and*
power-structure transformation), not in the clean H1/H2 test row. Venezuela is
the showcase of why the power-structure variable is indispensable — and it must
not be presented as clean evidence for H2.

## Conventions fixed

1. **Enforcement-mixed severance:** primary voluntariness coding follows the
   initiator; a patron's later sanctions enforcement is flagged (mixed), and
   the late phase is separately usable as imposed.
2. **Onset vs deepest years:** for gradual severances, the timing rule is
   reported separately for the episode's onset and its deepest phase, with
   external shocks (oil 2014) named explicitly.
