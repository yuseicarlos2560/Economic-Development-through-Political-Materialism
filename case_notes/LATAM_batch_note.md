## Rejections: pressure within the sphere is not severance

US sanctions/destabilization against its own clients are the sphere's
hierarchy functioning (Lake), not realignment — unless R2 families
actually terminate. **Convention made explicit (9):** F4 (patron coercion)
corroborates enforcement but is not itself an R2 family; it cannot
constitute a severance.
- **DOM 1960–64** (GSDB 61, 85): US/OAS sanctions on Trujillo 1960–61,
  coup politics, US intervention 1965. Zero families terminated by the DR;
  UN shows only mild drift (0.31 → 0.97 → 1.49, 1959–65). No episode.
  D025 (1964–65) = civil-war/intervention context.
- **HTI 1986–87** (GSDB 314, 343) and **HTI 2000–02** (GSDB 663; EU 675):
  aid pressure around Duvalier's fall and the Aristide disputes; Haiti
  never moved toward another patron; UN shows drift, no rupture. No
  episode. D042 (1988–96, −26.5%) = crisis/embargo context (the 1991–94
  embargo era was UN/OAS-sanctioned hierarchy enforcement ending in
  Aristide's restoration).
- **CRI 1999–2003** (GSDB 674, 715; share wobble 0.42→0.20→0.37):
  Intel-cycle trade oscillation plus minor pressure; no security/bloc
  change (CAFTA-DR 2009 deepened ties later). Noise — rejected.
