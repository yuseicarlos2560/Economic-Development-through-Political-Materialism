# Case note — Vietnam 1989–1995: severance absorbed, entry partial

**Episode IDs:** E008 (severance from USSR), E009 (US normalization — partial
entry) | **Status:** calibration episodes, in panel (VNM) | **Coder:** assistant
+ user, review pending | **Confidence:** E008 high; E009 **medium** (validation
flag — see below)

## E008 — Severance from the Soviet sphere

**1. Membership (R1), pre-1989.** Deep Soviet sphere: F1 — 1978 Treaty of
Friendship and Cooperation (ATOP 3825), Cam Ranh Bay base, advisors; F2 — Soviet
aid ≈ $1bn+/yr and subsidized CMEA trade; F3 — **CMEA member since 1978**.

**2. Severance (R2).**
- F2: Soviet aid cut from 1989; CMEA trade moves to world prices Jan 1991.
- F3: CMEA dissolved **June 1991**.
- F1: Soviet forces withdraw from Cam Ranh (announced 1989–90, completed 1992);
  the 1978 treaty lapses into a scaled-down friendship treaty in **1994**
  (ATOP 3825 ends 1994; succeeded by ATOP 4605, consul-only) — the formal tail
  of an already-effective severance.
Interval **[1989, 1991]**, with the 1994 treaty lapse noted as the formal tail.

**3. Voluntariness (R4).** **Imposed** — patron collapse (R4 first clause).
**4. R6: 1** (systemic shift).

**Validation:** UN ideal points — divergence from the patron 1989–91, and again
*patron-driven* (USSR −0.59 in 1989 → Russia +1.29 in 1992; Vietnam stays
~−1.3). Same signature as Cuba 1989–91. Passes.

**Outcome (post-hoc):** growth dips but stays positive (1989: +5.2%, 1990:
+2.9%, 1991: +3.8%) — **severance absorbed below threshold**, as registered in
`thresholds.md`. Why absorbed (for the case analysis, not the coding): Đổi Mới
reforms (1986) had already begun reorienting the economy toward East Asian
markets before the patron collapsed.

## E009 — US normalization 1994–95: entry, but of what?

**Record:** US trade embargo lifted **February 1994** (GSDB-R5 case 201 ends
1994; financial case 200 runs to 1998); diplomatic normalization **July 1995**;
ASEAN accession **July 1995** (F3, regional institution); bilateral trade
agreement **2000**. No US security ties, no bases, no alliance — F1 absent.

**The validation problem (recorded honestly):** UN ideal points show **no
convergence to the US** — Vietnam stays at ~−1.3 to −1.8 through 2000 while the
US is at ~+2.9. By the protocol's own cross-check, this does **not** look like
entry into the US *security sphere*, because it wasn't one: Vietnam integrated
into the US-led *economic order* while keeping full strategic autonomy (ruling
party, foreign-policy independence intact).

**Coding decision:** E009 is coded as entry, interval **[1994, 1995]** (embargo
lift + normalization + ASEAN), voluntariness **chosen**, R5 **richer** (US
39,391 vs East Asia median 2,167, Maddison 1995), R6 = 0 — but with
confidence **medium** and a **validation flag: UN-voting cross-check fails**.
Per the protocol, a failed cross-check does not erase the episode; it is
published with it. Vietnam refines the taxonomy: **economic-order entry without
security-sphere entry** is a distinct, codeable subtype.

**Outcome/timing honesty:** the acceleration episode (A069, 1992–2001, 6.1%/yr)
**precedes** the entry — by the timing rule it cannot be attributed to E009. It
follows Đổi Mới (1986), a domestic *policy* change that Protocol §4 and the
positive-cell note already classify as leaving the power structure untouched.
Vietnam therefore stands as: severance absorbed (threshold calibration), entry
partial (taxonomy refinement), acceleration home-grown (its attribution is
domestic reform, not realignment).

## Conventions fixed

1. **Validation failures are published, not fatal.** A coded episode whose UN
   cross-check fails carries a flag and lower confidence; it is not silently
   dropped or adjusted.
2. **Economic-order entry vs security-sphere entry** is an explicit subtype
   distinction (matters for interpretation of β₂ later).
3. Formal treaty lapses that trail the effective rupture (1978 treaty → 1994)
   are noted as the formal tail, not used to date the severance.
