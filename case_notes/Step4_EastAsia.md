# Step 4 full sweep — East Asia

**Scope:** JPN, KOR, PRK, VNM, MNG; 1945–2019. China is excluded from the design.
**Status:** substantive case determinations; user review requested; not merged into the panel.
**Continuity:** KOR follows the Republic of Korea, PRK the DPRK, and VNM the DRV/unified state. South Vietnamese acts are not imported into VNM.
**Non-contamination:** no economic outcome or expert-coded index was used.

## 2. Negative and sub-threshold determinations

- **JPN:** the 1955 party merger, repeated LDP succession, and the 1993/2009 alternations operate within the existing power structure. No second qualifying dimension accompanies those events; no later power episode is accepted.
- **KOR 1954:** constitutional amendment principally concerns executive/legislative arrangements within the First Republic; no independent second dimension is accepted beyond the 1952 episode. **1969:** third-term amendment is (d) only. **1979:** Park's assassination is turnover; the subsequent coup is coded in 1980. Post-1987 alternation, impeachment, and prosecution are operation of existing checks, not new transformation.
- **PRK:** 1954–62 amendments consolidate the founding order; 1992, 1998, 2009, 2012, 2013, 2016, and 2019 constitutional changes do not establish a new two-dimension effective transformation. Kim Jong-il's 1994 and Kim Jong-un's 2011 successions are turnover under the existing order; institutional renaming (NDC/SPA/State Affairs) is not enough.
- **VNM 1959/1960:** formal socialist replacement consolidates the DRV party-state; no new two-dimension effective event. **1975–78:** reunification extends the existing DRV order to the South but does not transform the panel state's domestic power structure; it is recorded as territorial/state consolidation and apparatus reorganization. **1980, 1992, 2001, 2013:** constitutional replacement/amendment and Đổi Mới policy change leave the effective Communist Party monopoly and control structure intact. No later power episode is accepted.
- **MNG 1960:** formal socialist consolidation, not a new effective transformation. **2001:** amendment does not meet two dimensions. **2019:** executive/parliamentary/judicial amendments principally support (a); no independent second dimension is accepted.

## 5. Verification limits

PRK's effective-power and apparatus findings carry a source-visibility caveat appropriate to the shadow case; dates come from formal instruments, while effective control is corroborated by the documented party/coercive hierarchy. VNM-01 concerns the founding of a new order and does not imply meaningful multiparty control after consolidation. Early apparatus cases use institutional histories where original decree scans are unavailable; no unverified annual persistence is converted into repeated events.
