# Case note — The Baltics 1990–2004: exit from incorporation, entry into the West

**Episode IDs:** E010–E012 (severance from USSR: EST, LVA, LTU), E013–E015
(entry into the US/EU sphere: EST, LVA, LTU) | **Status:** calibration episodes,
in panel | **Coder:** assistant + user, review pending | **Confidence:** high

One case note covers the three countries; the coding is identical, the
documentary dates nearly so. Deviations are noted per country.

## Coding note: incorporation, not just sphere membership

The Baltics were *annexed* (1940), not merely inside the Soviet sphere — the
extreme end of Lake's hierarchy. "Severance" here means exit from direct
control. This does not break the coding: the same four families apply, and the
terminations are all documented.

## E010–E012 — Severance from the USSR

**1. Membership (R1), pre-1990.** Maximal: full incorporation — F1 Soviet
forces stationed; F2 integrated into the all-Union economy (~80%+ of trade with
the USSR); F3 membership by annexation.

**2. Severance (R2).** Interval **[1990, 1994]**:
- 1990: independence declarations (LTU 11 March, EST 30 March, LVA 4 May);
  **Soviet blockade of Lithuania April–June 1990** (GSDB-R5 case 385 — the
  patron's coercion *against* the severance).
- 1991: independence restored/recognized (Aug–Sep 1991, UN admission 17 Sep);
  CMEA dissolved June 1991; USSR dissolved Dec 1991.
- 1992–93: ruble zone exit (EST Jun 1992, LTU Oct 1992, LVA 1992–93).
- 1993–94: Russian troops out (LTU Aug 1993; EST, LVA Aug 1994).
Terminations in F1 (troops), F2 (trade/monetary rupture), F3 (Union membership)
span 1990–1994 — beyond a single 3-year window, hence the interval code with
the core rupture in 1990–91.

**3. Voluntariness (R4).** **Chosen** — the initiative predates the patron's
collapse (declarations March–May 1990; the blockade failed to stop them). The
patron then ceased to exist (Dec 1991). Coded *chosen* with a flag: also usable
in the imposed-robustness variant, since the patron's collapse overlapped.
(New convention: chosen-with-collapse = mixed flag; primary coding follows who
initiated.)

**4. R5 n/a. R6: 1** (systemic shift).

## E013–E015 — Entry into the US/EU sphere

**1. Entry (R3).** Interval **[1995, 2004]**:
- Jun 1995: EU Europe Agreements signed (F2/F3 institutional establishment;
  trade had already redirected West — Nordic free-trade treaties from 1992,
  ATOP 4241/4280/4285 et al.).
- **26 Mar 2003**: NATO accession (ATOP 3180, defense=1 — the security
  guarantee; full membership 29 Mar 2004).
- **1 May 2004**: EU membership (F3 bloc).
De facto alignment precedes the formal dates: UN voting is Western from the
first session.

**3. Voluntariness (R4).** **Chosen**, unambiguously (referenda, applications).
**4. Sign (R5).** US GDP pc 2004 = 48,493 vs post-Soviet median = 7,782
(Maddison) → richer. R6: 0.

## Validation

- **UN ideal points:** the Baltics enter the UN (Sep 1991) already at ~1.2 —
  between Russia (0.73) and the US (2.93) and far from their Soviet-era
  position — and hold ~1.0–1.45 through 2004 while Russia falls away
  (0.99 → −0.20 by 2002). Divergence from the patron: immediate. Convergence to
  the West: present from day one, partial in level (they never reach 2.9, as
  expected — the US anchor sits at the pole).

## Outcome check (post-hoc)

- **Severance → disruption, timing satisfied:** EST −28.2% (1990–95), LVA
  −41.4% (1990–96), LTU −43.2% (1990–96); contraction phases 1990/91–1993/94,
  i.e., following the 1990 declarations and the 1991 rupture. H2 negative sign
  confirmed in all three.
- **Entry → acceleration, timing satisfied via the interval:** accelerations
  begin 1998 (EST 1998–2007 at 6.8%, LVA 1998–2008 at 8.4%, LTU 1998–2009 at
  7.0%) — inside the entry interval [1995, 2004], following the Europe
  Agreements. H2 positive sign confirmed, with the interval convention doing
  the work (a point coding at 2004 would have failed the timing rule; the
  interval records that entry was a process).
- The 2007–10 disruptions (EST −18.6%, LVA −18.0%) are the global financial
  crisis — a year-fixed-effects matter, not a realignment; noted here so they
  are not mistaken for severance effects later.

## Conventions fixed

1. **Chosen-with-collapse** voluntariness: primary coding follows who initiated;
   a mixed flag keeps the episode usable in the imposed-only robustness variant.
2. **Entry intervals may bracket an acceleration** when the institutional
   establishment (Europe Agreements 1995) precedes consolidation (2004);
   the timing rule is evaluated against the interval's start.
