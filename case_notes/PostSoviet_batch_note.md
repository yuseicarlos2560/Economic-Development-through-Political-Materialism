## No-episode countries (R1: sphere continuity with Russia throughout)

- **BLR — Russian sphere continuously.** UN distance to Russia 0.28–0.7
  (1995–2019); Russia trade share 46–59% (2000–2019); CST 1993 → CSTO 2002
  → EAEU 2015; Union State treaty 1996/1999. Screen candidates (1991–93,
  2015 EAEU) **rejected: continuation signals, not change** — entry into
  successor institutions of the same sphere is not a realignment.
- **ARM — Russian sphere continuously.** UN distance 0.02–0.48 (near-zero);
  CST 1992 → CSTO 2002 → EAEU 2015; Gyumri base (1995, extended 2010 to
  2044). Screen candidates (1991–93, 1996–97, 2015) rejected: continuation.
- **KAZ — Russian sphere continuously.** UN distance 0.2–0.7; Russia share
  20–28%; CST/CSTO, EAEU 2015 (Nazarbayev an architect), Baikonur lease.
  Screen candidates (1991–96, 1996–98, 1999–2001, 2013–15) rejected:
  continuation. The 1990s multi-family events are CIS/CST accession and
  Russian pressure cases (GSDB), not termination.
- **KGZ — Russian sphere continuously.** CST/CSTO, EAEU 2015, Kant base
  (2003). Screen candidates (1991–93, 2007, 2015) rejected: continuation.
- **TJK — Russian sphere continuously.** CST/CSTO, 201st base (extended
  2012 to 2042); EAEU never joined — noted, does not meet a change rule.
  Screen candidates rejected: continuation.

## Contested but sub-threshold (no ≥2-family termination in any window)

- **MDA — contested reorientation, not consummated by 2019.** CIS 1994;
  Russian troops in Transnistria remain through 2019 (F1 never terminated);
  ruble exit 1993; EU Association Agreement 2014 (documented, unconsummated
  entry per E017 convention). Screen candidate (2000–01, F1+F2) rejected:
  treaty continuation + trade fluctuation, no termination act. Confidence
  medium — flagged for user review.
- **UZB — oscillation within the Russian sphere.** CST non-renewal 1999
  (single family; GUAM); US K2 base 2001–05 = failed Western opening
  (context); CSTO 2006–12 (re-entry then suspension — again single family
  per window); never EAEU; CIS throughout. No window reaches ≥2 terminated
  families → no severance episode. Screen candidates (1991–93, 1997–99,
  2002–03, 2004–07, 2012) rejected accordingly. Confidence medium-high.
- **RUS — the patron.** Screen candidate RUS–USA 1991–92 rejected: US
  sanctions on the dissolving USSR (GSDB 422) and treaty accessions are the
  patron's own transition, not a realignment of a sphere member. RUS
  carries the R6 context, not an episode.
