## The four judgments (CP §6.1)

**1. Membership (R1).** Pre-1945: none was in a Soviet sphere (the interwar
period had no Soviet alliance system; prewar security arrangements ran to
Western powers or neighbors). Entry from non-membership — satisfied.

**2. Change (R3 — entry).** ≥2 families within 3 years:
- F1: bilateral friendship treaties with the USSR — CZE Dec 1943 (renewed
  1963; ATOP 2563 running to 1970), POL Apr 1945 (3025), HUN 1948 (3120),
  ROU 1948 (3115), BGR 1948 (3135). The Warsaw Pact 1955 (3285)
  multilateralizes the guarantee — formal tail, not the entry act.
- F3: Cominform Sep 1947 (party-bloc; E001 convention counts it as a bloc
  family); CMEA founded Jan 1949 (USSR + POL, HUN, ROU, BGR, CZE; ALB joins
  Feb 1949).
- F2: reparations, joint-stock companies (ROU SovRoms; HUN), the CMEA
  transfer system. Documented honestly: in the early years the net flow runs
  patron-ward (extraction); the subsidization direction reverses only later
  (raw-material pricing from the 1960s).
All three families established by 1949 for all seven.

**3. Voluntariness (R4).** **Imposed** for E034–E039: entries under Soviet
military occupation with communist takeovers (POL rigged 1947 elections;
HUN salami tactics 1947–48; ROU imposed Groza government 1945, monarchy
abolished Dec 1947; BGR Fatherland Front takeover 1944–46; CZE Feb 1948
coup). Imposed entries carry causal weight under R4 exactly as imposed
severances do — first use of the class; registered as a convention below.
**E040 ALB is the exception: CHOSEN** — indigenous partisan victory (Nov
1944, no Soviet troops on Albanian soil) and voluntary ideological alignment
with the USSR. Flagged inside the block; the same country's 1960–68
severance (E047) is likewise chosen.
