# Step 4 full sweep — post-Soviet countries

**Scope:** RUS, UKR, BLR, EST, LVA, LTU, MDA, GEO, ARM, AZE, KAZ, KGZ, TJK, TKM, UZB; 1945–2019.
**Status:** substantive case determinations; user review requested; not merged into the panel.
**Sequence/non-contamination:** CCP candidates and legal/institutional records were reviewed without economic outcomes or expert regime indices. Russia inherits the USSR record. Other successor states use their own republican and independence records rather than importing USSR domestic events.

## Rules exercised

- A foundational transition is coded once even when its constitution consummates an already-open transformation. The effective year is the second qualifying dimension, not automatically the constitution year.
- Party–state change includes movement from a single-party order to competing parties or a successor state party; it does not imply party–state separation or democratic completion.
- Direct election, parliamentary selection, abolition of term limits, and executive–legislative reallocation are distinct only when their substantive effects are independently identified.
- Routine succession, operation of existing courts, vertical reallocation, and one-dimension amendments do not meet the power-structure threshold.
- Foundational apparatus episodes require named transfers or creations of durable machinery. Monetary policy and fiscal policy alone do not qualify; national monetary/fiscal machinery and coercive reorganization do.

## 1. Power-structure determinations

The shared foundational dimension (c) is the effective end of the Communist Party's constitutional/organizational monopoly through republican constitutional amendments, party-legalization acts, and competitive elections. Dimension (d) is the new effective capacity to replace the chief executive through competitive election. Neither is a claim that the result satisfied Trevijano's representation and separation criteria.

### Rejected or deferred power-structure candidates

- **RUS 2000–05:** federal-district, Federation-Council, and gubernatorial-selection changes establish (a), but vertical reallocation is not (b), and control of local rulers is not automatically national (d). A possible United Russia (c) contribution remains **conditional** on durable organizational party–state integration evidence; no episode is accepted on that basis here. RUS 2008 (term length) is (d) only; RUS 2014 territorial changes do not qualify.
- **UKR 2016:** judicial reform is one effective domain and cannot alone meet the threshold. UKR 2019 produces no second distinct dimension.
- **BLR 2004:** removal of the presidential two-term limit is (d) only. The 1994 election itself is not relabelled as a further 1995–96 control change.
- **EST/LVA/LTU post-founding amendments:** EU-related, franchise, language, and routine institutional amendments do not by themselves establish two dimensions. No separate post-1993 power episode is accepted.
- **MDA 1996, 2001–03, 2017–18:** no second distinct dimension is established in a three-year window beyond the separately coded 2000 and 2016 changes. Electoral-system changes require a demonstrated effect on direct/territorial representation or party dependence, not merely a changed formula.
- **GEO 2017–18:** the shift to indirect presidential election is principally effective in 2024, outside the coding window; no new in-window two-dimension episode is inferred from promulgation. Earlier judicial amendments are not relabelled as a second dimension.
- **ARM 2005:** executive–legislative and referendum changes principally support (a); no independent second dimension is accepted. ARM 2018 mass mobilization and turnover are not an additional legal transformation after PS-ARM-02.
- **AZE 2002:** succession and related changes do not establish a new control capacity under the no-relabelling rule. **AZE 2009:** abolition of the presidential term limit is (d) only.
- **KAZ 1998 and 2007:** presidential term/eligibility changes are (d) only. **KAZ 2017:** redistribution of executive/legislative functions is (a) only. KAZ 2019 is turnover under existing rules.
- **KGZ 2016:** strengthened government/judicial arrangements do not establish a second distinct dimension; no new episode.
- **TJK 2003 and 2016:** presidential term/continuity changes are (d) only. The 2015/2016 Leader of the Nation framework does not supply an independently evidenced second dimension.
- **TKM 1999:** lifetime presidency is (d) only. **TKM 2008:** formal separation and bicameral/parliamentary changes are not sufficient proof of effective separation; no second effective dimension is accepted. **TKM 2016:** term/age rules are (d) only. The 2006 succession operated under existing rules.
- **UZB 2002:** referendum term change is (d) only. **UZB 2011/2014:** stronger prime-minister/parliament roles support (a) only. The 2016 succession is not a transformation.

## 3. Formal anchors and candidate cautions

Principal `RegimeChange` anchors are the constitutional replacements, suspensions/restorations, executive-selection amendments, and electoral-access/organization changes identified in the country records above. The CCP ledger remains the candidate census; its `new` or `amendment` label is not itself a positive code.

- **RUS:** 1977 replacement; 1988–92 competitive-election, presidency, and sovereignty amendments; 1993 replacement; 2008 presidential/parliamentary term amendment. The 2014 territorial amendment is not treated as a change in national access to power.
- **UKR:** 1991 independence/presidential rules; 1996 replacement; 2004 amendment; 2010 judicial annulment; 2014 restoration; 2016 judicial-organization amendment. Later technical/language amendments require content review rather than automatic inclusion.
- **BLR:** 1994 replacement; 1996 and 2004 amendments.
- **EST/LVA/LTU:** restoration acts in 1990–91 are included even where CCP records only the 1991/1992 constitutional event; EST 1992, LVA 1991/1993, LTU 1992 are the principal replacement/restoration anchors.
- **MDA/GEO/ARM/AZE/KAZ/KGZ/TJK/TKM/UZB:** independence/executive-selection acts and their 1990s constitutions qualify; the later amendments accepted or rejected above are decided by content, not by CCP event type.
- **GEO 1978:** this is the Georgian SSR's own formal record, not an imported USSR event. It is retained as a candidate but is not evidence by itself of an effective post-Soviet transformation.

### Verification limits

The accepted episodes rest on named legal/institutional acts, but not every linked institutional history is itself the text of the original act. AR-TKM-01 is therefore medium confidence; AR-GEO-01, AR-AZE-01, AR-KAZ-01, AR-TJK-01, and AR-UZB-01 retain a source-precision caveat. These caveats affect source grade, not the binary threshold, because the existence and effective years of the two components are independently documented. No conditional power-structure episode is promoted to accepted status without the missing organizational evidence.
