## Convention: deepening is not entry

Accession to EU/NATO by a country already inside the Western sphere is
**intra-sphere deepening**, not an entry episode (R1 fails: no *new*
sphere). Applied:
- **ESP 1986 EU** — already inside per E002 (1953 entry). Deepening.
- **GRC 1981 EU** — inside since E032 (1947–52). Deepening.
- **PRT 1986 EU** — inside since E031. Deepening (the 1974–76
  democratization is a power-structure event for Step 4, not a realignment).
- **DNK / IRL / GBR 1973 EEC** — inside since the formation block (IRL via
  OEEC/Marshall F2+F3 membership from 1948; never NATO — F1 absent
  throughout, consistent across the record). Deepening.
- **AUT / SWE 1995 EU** — inside the Western economic order throughout
  (OEEC 1948; EFTA); no security-sphere change (neither joins NATO in the
  window; AUT never). Deepening. **CHE**: never accedes (EFTA/bilateral);
  no episode at any point.
- **NOR 1994 EEA** — NATO member since 1949; EEA is a substitute for EU
  membership (referendum rejected EU, Nov 1994), not a sphere move. No
  episode.
- **CYP 2004 EU** — association agreement 1972, customs-union protocol
  1987: already inside the Western economic order; NAM membership was not a
  rival sphere; no security-sphere change (outside NATO throughout; British
  sovereign bases unchanged). Deepening. Borderline pair with E059 MLT —
  see the entry note for the discrimination and the review flag.
- **FRA 1966** — withdrawal from NATO's *integrated military command*, not
  from the alliance: single-family partial move, no family terminated (F1
  treaty intact, F2/F3 untouched). Recorded as nuance, no episode.

## Convention: succession is not sphere severance

Yugoslavia left the Soviet sphere in 1948 (E001) and was non-aligned
thereafter — not a sphere holder. The 1991 Yugoslav dissolution is a
**state succession**, not a sphere severance: no SVN/HRV severance episode
is coded. Their collapses are recorded as context: HRV D041 (1989–95,
−33.5%, war), SVN D077/D078 (war aftermath; D3 hyperinflation 1281%,
Yugoslav dinar). Their Western entries are coded as entries (E055, E056).
