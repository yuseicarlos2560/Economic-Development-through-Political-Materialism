# Case note — North Korea–USSR 1990–1991 (severance, E073 — shadow case)

**Episode ID:** E073 | **Status:** Phase B batch 4 (East Asia), shadow case
(outcome estimates only — Bank of Korea reconstructions) | **Coder:**

## Prior status (initial condition, documented)

DPRK in the Soviet sphere from 1945 (Soviet occupation of the north; state
founded 1948; war 1950–53; postwar reconstruction aid). The **1961 dual
treaties** formalize the position: USSR–DPRK Treaty (ATOP 3440, defense=1,
1961–1996) and China–DPRK Treaty (ATOP 3445, defense=1, **still active**).
Per CP §6.2, China before 2013 is not a coded patron: the 1961 China treaty
and the Sino-Soviet balancing era are documented here as context — with the
explicit note that 3445 is the extant Chinese defense commitment, which is
why the shadow case had to exist (user decision, Phase A).

## The four judgments (CP §6.1)

**R1.** Satisfied (F1 treaty + troops/arms; F2 concessional trade and aid —
the dominant external flows into the 1980s).

**R2 — severance.** ≥2 families terminated within 3 years, and unusually the
**patron initiated the break**:
- F2: USSR–ROK normalization 30 Sep 1990; Soviet aid terminated; trade
  switched to hard currency/world prices Jan 1991; Soviet trade collapses
  ~70% in 1991–93 (documented; DOTS shows only residuals ~0.04–0.10 with a
  1996 artifact — recorded).
- F1: the security relationship ends in substance with the patron's
  dissolution (Dec 1991); the 1961 treaty (ATOP 3440) lapses formally in
  1996 = formal tail (E001 convention); a weak successor treaty follows in
  2000 (ATOP 4940, no defense obligation).
Interval **[1990, 1991]**: earliest = USSR–ROK normalization + aid
termination; latest = dissolution.

**R4.** **Imposed** — patron initiative *and* patron collapse: the client
protested and did not move. The cleanest imposed case of the batch.

**R6 = 1.**
