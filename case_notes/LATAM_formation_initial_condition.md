# Case note — Latin America 1947–48: initial condition, not a formation block

**Status:** Phase B batch 3 (Latin America) | **Coder:** assistant + user,
review pending | **Resolves:** 20 screen candidates (ARG, BOL, BRA, CHL, COL,
CRI, CUB, DOM, ECU [1947–49], GTM, HND, HTI, MEX, NIC, PAN, PER, PRY, SLV,
URY, VEN — all flagged "mixed(entry+severance)" for 1947–48/49)

## The finding

The screen sees: ATOP 3010 exits 1947 → ATOP 3075 enters 1947 (Rio Treaty)
+ ATOP 3150 enters 1948 (OAS Charter, coded defense obligation) + OAS bloc
entry 1948. Read as realignment, this looks like an exit-and-entry. It is
not one: **ATOP 3010 is the Act of Chapultepec (1945)**, the wartime
inter-American defense arrangement, replaced by its peacetime successors.
The sequence is F1 *continuity* across an institutionalization:

- F1: Act of Chapultepec 1945 (3010) → Rio Treaty Sep 1947 (3075) +
  OAS Charter 1948 (3150). No gap in the security guarantee.
- F3: OAS founded Bogotá 1948 — the formal bloc of an existing system.
- F2: US trade dominance already in place at the window edge (CUB–USA
  0.64–0.73 in 1948–57 confirmed in Phase A; the same pattern holds across
  the region's exports).

## Ruling (R1 applied)

R1 for an entry episode requires the country *not* to be a member of the
sphere at interval start. Latin America in 1945 was already inside the
US-led informal sphere — the paradigmatic informal sphere of the theory's
own literature (Keal/Hast): Monroe Doctrine, Panama 1903, the Platt-
Amendment protectorate system, Dollar Diplomacy, Good Neighbor, wartime
cooperation (Act of Havana 1940, Chapultepec 1945). The 1947–48 acts
**institutionalize a pre-existing membership** — they are the region's
deepening/formalization moment, exactly the class the deepening convention
(batch 2, convention 1) excludes.

**Decision:** no formation episodes. The sphere roster carries all 20
countries as **US-sphere members from 1945 (initial condition)**. This is
consistent with, not contrary to, the European formation blocks: in Europe
the 1947–49 acts constituted *new* memberships (wartime alliance ≠
peacetime sphere); here the membership pre-existed the window and the acts
merely formalized it. The difference is substantive, documented, and
registered here before Step 6.

## Consequences

- CUB's initial condition is US-sphere membership from 1945 — which is
  exactly what E003's severance (1960–62) already presupposes. Consistent.
- ECU joins the Rio Treaty in 1949 (ATOP yrent 1949) — same class, no
  episode.
- What would falsify this ruling: a country demonstrably *outside* the US
  sphere in 1945–47 then joining it. None exists in the record (even
  Perón's Argentina acceded to Chapultepec, Rio and the OAS on schedule).
- The decolonization entries (TTO 1967, JAM 1969, SUR 1977, GUY 1991) are
  handled separately — those ARE new sovereign accessions to the system
  (`case_notes/Caribbean_entries.md`).

## Convention registered (new, user ratification pending)

7. **Pre-existing informal sphere membership at the window edge = roster
   initial condition, not an episode.** Formalization/institutionalization
   of an existing membership is deepening, not entry — the mirror image of
   window-edge convention 3 (Europe).
