# Step 4 full sweep — Latin America

**Scope:** all 24 Latin American panel countries, 1945–2019.
**Status:** substantive case determinations; user review requested; not merged into the panel.
**Non-contamination:** coding uses CCP candidates, constitutional texts, legal acts, and institutional records only. No economic outcome or expert regime index was consulted.

## Coding conventions exercised

- A coup is not automatically a power-structure event. It qualifies only where the record shows two effective dimensions, commonly (a) displacement/concentration of institutional authority plus (d) removal of competitive or constitutional control; party bans/fusion may independently add (c).
- A democratic restoration is a separate effective change when party competition and replacement become operative. Formal constitutional reinstatement alone is insufficient.
- A new constitution under an unchanged effective order is `RegimeChange` without a new `PowerStructure` episode.
- Independence episodes establish partial change from colonial rule, not a claim that the resulting order meets Trevijano's ideal.
- Public enterprise is not automatically core apparatus. PDVSA qualifies on the fiscal/extraction side only because durable state revenue/extraction functions and their organizational control were restructured; the same act is not also counted as administrative machinery.

## 2. Rejected and sub-threshold candidates

- **ARG:** 1956/1957 repeal of the Peronist constitution is formal change inside the post-1955 reversal, not an additional episode; 1970–72 statutes belong to the continuing military order; routine impeachment/succession after 1983 is not transformation.
- **BOL:** 1945/1947/1961/1967 formal constitutions are not separate effective events where the surrounding coup/revolution already carries the change; 1994–2005 electoral/administrative amendments do not meet two dimensions; 2016 rejection of re-election is operation of a referendum rule, not a new transformation.
- **BRA:** 1950/1956 amendments are routine; 1997 re-election is (d) only; 2016 impeachment operated under existing constitutional rules.
- **CHL:** 1957–71 electoral reforms principally concern access/(b), without a second dimension; 1980 is the junta's formal consummation; 2011–19 amendments do not establish a two-dimension event.
- **COL:** 1968–86 amendments do not meet the threshold; 2004/2005 re-election is (d) only; later electoral and peace-implementation changes require evidence of two effective dimensions and are not accepted here.
- **CRI:** 1949 Constitution consummates PS-CRI-01; subsequent electoral, rights, EU/trade, and administrative amendments do not meet the threshold.
- **CUB:** 1953 formal restoration did not undo Batista's effective order; 1976, 1992, 2002, and 2019 formal changes do not demonstrate a new two-dimension effective transformation. Leadership succession in 2008/2018 is turnover, not structural change.
- **DOM:** 1947/1955 constitutions consolidate Trujillo's existing order; 2010 judicial/institutional changes principally support (a); 2015 re-election is (d) only.
- **ECU:** 1961 is succession/turnover without a separately established two-dimension change; 1984/1993/1996/1997 CCP `new` labels require content caution and are not automatically separate transformations; 2015 indefinite re-election is (d) only.
- **SLV:** 1950 and 1983 constitutions formalize existing military orders; the 1992 peace settlement, not the annual constitutional amendments alone, supplies the threshold evidence.
- **GTM:** 1956/1965 constitutions consolidate preceding military orders; the failed 1993 autogolpe and rapid restoration are not accepted as a durable two-dimension transformation.
- **GUY:** 1970 republican status did not alter effective control; 1992 elections demonstrate operation of competition, not a new rule; later amendments do not meet the threshold.
- **HTI:** 1950/1957 and 1971 formal/succession changes do not independently meet two dimensions; 2012 amendment does not establish a second dimension.
- **HND:** 1957/1965 constitutions are anchors around the already-coded military orders; 2012–13 re-election litigation is not relabelled as two dimensions.
- **JAM:** 1971–2017 amendments do not produce a two-dimension effective transformation; party alternation is routine control.
- **MEX:** 1946–93 electoral changes alone do not all qualify; 1977 opposition/access reform is (b) only; 2000 alternation is operation of the transformed order; 2014 IFE-to-INE change is principally (a), without a second dimension.
- **NIC:** 1948/1950/1974 constitutions consolidate the Somoza order; 1987 formalizes the post-1979 order; 2000/2004/2005/2007 electoral or executive changes do not each meet the threshold; the 2010 court ruling is not counted as two dimensions by itself.
- **PAN:** 1956–64 amendments do not meet the threshold; 1983/1993/1994/2004 changes do not establish two effective dimensions.
- **PRY:** 1967 consolidates Stroessner's order; 1977 re-election is (d) only; the CCP 2011 amendment is not accepted without a demonstrated access/organization change.
- **PER:** 1988–91 amendments do not meet the threshold; 2000 electoral/term issues are not a new two-dimension episode; later amendments do not establish one either.
- **SUR:** 1981/1982 interim acts belong to the military order; 1992 amendment does not create a second transformation after PS-SUR-05.
- **TTO:** 1976 republican replacement did not change effective party/electoral control; later amendments remain sub-threshold.
- **URY:** 1976–83 junta amendments belong to the 1973 military order; 1989/1994/1996 electoral changes do not establish two dimensions; 2004 fiscal/electoral amendments do not qualify.
- **VEN:** 1953 consolidates military rule; 1961 formalizes the post-1958 order; 1999 is formal `RegimeChange` but not automatically a second-dimension transformation; 2013 enabling law is (a) only and Maduro's succession is turnover.

## 5. Verification limits and review flags

- The repeated-coup apparatus records use the same substantive transfer test, but several early episodes rely on constitutional and institutional histories rather than scans of every implementing decree. Their medium-high confidence reflects source precision, not an exemption from the two-component rule.
- **AR-GUY-02** is **medium/conditional pending a more precise second-component act**; it is not to enter the accepted panel build without that anchor.
- PS-MEX-01 rests on the combined electoral-administration, judicial, and party–state record, not on the 2000 outcome. The effective date remains 1996.
- PS-VEN-04 begins in 2000 but crosses its (a)+(c) threshold in 2003; PS-VEN-05 crosses in 2009. The 2004 TSJ law is not falsely used to make a 2004–09 six-year pairing.
- CCP records that appear anomalous (including ECU 1984/1993/1996 and PRY 2011) remain candidates, never automatic positive formal or effective codes.
