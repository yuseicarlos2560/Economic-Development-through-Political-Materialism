# Case note — Greece 2015: the threatened, unconsummated severance

**Episode ID:** E017 | **Status:** calibration episode, in panel (GRC); the
negative control | **Coder:** assistant + user, review pending |
**Confidence:** high

## What 2015 was — and was not

The Syriza government (Jan 2015) threatened exit from the **eurozone** — the
monetary union, a sub-sphere institution, not the EU and not the US sphere.
Sequence: bailout expiry and capital controls **28 June 2015**, referendum
rejecting the creditors' terms **5 July 2015** (61%), then the third MoU
**August 2015** — Greece stayed. No termination was consummated in any
indicator family.

## Coding against R2

- F1 security: untouched (NATO member continuously since 1951, ATOP 3180).
- F2 economic: no trade/aid rupture; the ELA freeze (June 2015) was reversed
  with the August MoU.
- F3 bloc: no exit — from neither the eurozone, the EU, nor NATO.
- F4: no sanctions (GSDB has nothing against Greece in 2015).
Zero families terminated → **not a severance**. Coded as **severance_threat,
unconsummated**, per CP §6's calibration plan. The 3-year window and ≥2-family
rules are exactly what prevent a dramatic political year from being coded as a
realignment it never became.

## Validation

- **UN ideal points:** Greece holds ~0.9–1.2, side by side with Germany
  (~0.9–1.35), throughout 2008–2019 — no divergence even in 2015. The threat
  left no trace in revealed alignment: the negative control is clean.

## Outcome check (post-hoc)

2015 growth: **+0.4%** — no new disruption; the screen coded none. The existing
Greek disruption (D031, 2006–2015, −26.2%, contraction phase 2009–13) follows
the eurozone crisis, *precedes* the 2015 threat, and by the timing rule cannot
be attributed to it. The negative control works in both directions: no
consummated severance, no new disruption.

## Conventions fixed

1. **Threat flag:** a threatened but unconsummated severance is coded
   `severance_threat` with `unconsummated` — it enters the episode table
   (falsification design needs the near-misses) but is excluded from the
   Severance regressor; robustness may include it.
2. **Sub-sphere nuance:** the threatened object (eurozone, not EU/US sphere) is
   recorded in the notes; a eurozone exit would have coded F3 termination with
   the patron relationship unchanged.
