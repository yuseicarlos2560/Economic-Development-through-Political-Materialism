## E048 — severance [1991, 1992]

**R1.** Finland was never a satellite, but the 1948 YYA treaty (Treaty of
Friendship, Cooperation and Mutual Assistance, ATOP 3140) — with its
military-consultation articles — plus the trade dependence (~20–25% of
exports to the USSR at the mid-1980s peak, bilateral clearing) placed it
inside the Soviet sphere's constraint set. Coded membership on F1+F2. The
"Finlandization" nuance (a constrained democracy, not a bloc member) is why
the interval end is defined by the treaty's replacement, not by any bloc
exit.

**R2.** F1 terminated: the USSR dissolved Dec 1991; the YYA was replaced by
the 20 Jan 1992 treaty with Russia with **all military articles dropped**.
F2 terminated: Soviet trade collapsed 1991 (the clearing system ended; the
~20–25% export share fell to low single digits by 1992–93). Two families
within 1991–92. Interval **[1991, 1992]**.

**R4.** **Imposed (patron collapse)** — Finland did not initiate; the patron
state dissolved and the successor state signed away the military articles.
E005 first clause.

**R6 = 1.**
