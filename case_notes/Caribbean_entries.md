## E067 GUY [1985, 1991] — medium confidence, flagged for user review

Guyana is the exception of the four: independence 1966 was NOT followed by
accession. Burnham's cooperative socialism took Guyana into non-alignment:
nationalizations (bauxite 1971–76), NAM activism, Cuba ties 1972, and a
long UN-corroborated drift toward the Soviet position (GUY–USSR distance
2.86 [1970] → 0.01 [1988]) — **without consummation**: no Soviet security
guarantee, no CMEA, no aid program (single-family rhetorical/economic
ties at most → no Soviet entry coded, same rule as PER 1968–75 and SUR
1982–83 in the batch note). Western economic ties persisted throughout
(IMF program 1978; bauxite to market economies) — the tension this medium
confidence rests on: Guyana was formally non-aligned (non-membership) but
never fully left the Western economic order either.

The entry proper: Hoyte succeeds Burnham (Aug 1985) and reverses course —
IMF/World Bank economic recovery program 1988, OAS accession Jan 1991
(ATOP 3150; NOT the Rio Treaty). **Consummation rule applied:** the OAS
Charter accession supplies F1 (collective-security articles) + F3 (bloc)
— security-track consummation at 1991. Interval **[1985, 1991]**: earliest
= Hoyte's reorientation; latest = the OAS act. R5 richer ✓ (1991).

**Validation:** GUY–USSR diverges 0.01 (1988) → 1.32 (1991); GUY–USA
converges from 3.67 (1990) → 3.10 (1993). Passes, and corroborates the
1985–91 window rather than the 1966 independence date.
