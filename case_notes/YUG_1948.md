# Case note — Yugoslavia–USSR 1948–1949 (severance by expulsion)

**Episode ID:** E001 | **Status:** calibration episode (Yugoslavia is not in the
73-country panel; coded to fix conventions, per Coding_Protocol §6) | **Coder:**
assistant + user, review pending | **Confidence:** high

## The four judgments (CP §6.1)

**1. Membership (R1).** Yugoslavia inside the Soviet sphere from 1945:
- F1 security: Treaty of Friendship, Mutual Assistance and Postwar Cooperation
  with the USSR, April 1945 (ATOP treaty 3020; defense=1, offense=1 obligations).
- F3 bloc membership: founding member of the Cominform, September 1947.
- F2 economic: reparations, joint-stock companies, and trade concentrated on the
  bloc (~50% of Yugoslav foreign trade with the USSR/Eastern Europe pre-1948;
  historical estimate — DOTS too sparse pre-1960 to confirm numerically).
≥2 families satisfied (in fact three). Membership: yes, Soviet sphere.

**2. Change (R2 — severance).** Termination of ≥2 of {security guarantee,
subsidized trade/aid, bloc membership} within 3 years:
- F3: **June 1948** — Cominform resolution expels the Communist Party of
  Yugoslavia (28 June 1948).
- F2: 1948–1949 — USSR recalls specialists, cancels credits; bloc trade collapses
  to near zero by 1949. GSDB-R5 case 21 codes the complete trade embargo
  (exp_compl, imp_compl) from **March 1950** to March 1955 (the formal record;
  the rupture is de facto 1948–49).
- F1: **September 1949** — USSR formally denounces the 1945 Friendship Treaty
  (ATOP: yrexit 1949, term=renounced).
All three families terminate within June 1948 – September 1949 (< 3 years).
Interval-coded **[1948, 1949]**: earliest defensible = 1948 (expulsion + de facto
embargo); latest = 1949 (formal treaty denunciation). The GSDB embargo start
(1950-03) is noted as the formal sanctions record, not the severance date.

**3. Voluntariness (R4).** **Imposed.** Yugoslavia was expelled; Stalin initiated
the break and enforced it with the embargo (F4 coercion family: GSDB case 21).
Tito did not choose exit — the resolution targeted the Yugoslav leadership while
calling on it to reverse course. Imposed severance → carries causal weight.

**4. Sign (R5).** Not applicable to severance. **R6 systemic-shift flag: 0**
(1948–49 is bloc consolidation within the emerging bipolar order, not a change
in the global distribution of capabilities).

## Validation

- **UN ideal points (Bailey–Strezhnev–Voeten):** Yugoslavia vs USSR nearly
  identical 1946–48 (−3.05 vs −3.06 in 1946; −2.87 vs −2.88 in 1948), then sharp
  divergence from 1949: gap 1.1 (1949), 1.8 (1950), 2.0 (1951), 2.2 (1952).
  The cross-check passes — coded severance coincides with voting divergence.
- **Timing rule:** outcomes (had Yugoslavia been in the panel) would only count
  if post-1948/49. Not applicable here (calibration only).

## Conventions fixed by this episode

1. Bloc-membership termination (Cominform/CMEA-type) counts as a full family,
  even when the body is political rather than military.
2. The severance interval is anchored to documentary events (expulsion,
  denunciation), not to the sanctions database's formal start date when the
  latter lags the de facto rupture; the lag is noted, not hidden.
3. Expulsion by the patron codes as *imposed* under R4 — the clearest case of
  the class.
